## 4. The Problem

Many crypto projects face the same recurring issues:

**Uncontrolled Supply**  
Projects with mint functions can inflate supply at any time, harming holders.

**Liquidity Risk**  
Unlocked liquidity and unchecked issuer accounts create significant holder risk.

**Excessive Team Control**  
Large unrestricted team allocations create selling pressure and erode trust.

**Lack of Transparency**  
Holders often cannot verify key project data on-chain.

**Short-Term Focus**  
Hype-driven projects fail to build sustainable communities or long-term value.

---

## 5. The ELARA Solution

ELARA is designed to solve these problems at the foundation level.

### Fixed Supply — Permanently
ELARA has a total supply of exactly:

**100,000,000,000 ELARA**

This is the maximum supply that will ever exist. Not a single additional ELARA token can be created — ever.

### Issuer Account Permanently Locked
The ELARA issuer account master weight has been set to **0**. This is a one-way, irreversible action recorded permanently on the Stellar blockchain.

This means:
- No new ELARA tokens can be minted
- The issuer cannot modify token properties
- No single entity — including the founding team — controls issuance

This can be independently verified by anyone at any time.

### Stellar Blockchain
ELARA is built on Stellar — a proven, energy-efficient, and fast blockchain with ~3-5 second transaction finality and near-zero fees.

### Soroban Smart Contract
ELARA is deployed as a Stellar Asset Contract (SAC) on Soroban — Stellar's smart contract platform — enabling future DeFi and ecosystem integrations.
